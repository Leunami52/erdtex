What makes a GSM diagram (graph-based semantic model) correct: the rules behind the Problems panel.

In these rules, **must** means the program reports an **error**, **should** a **warning**, and **may**
that something is allowed. Each problem in the Problems panel names its rule, and its context menu
opens it here.
