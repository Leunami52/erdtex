+++
id = "ER-26"
title = "Depending on itself, one end must allow at most one"
kind = "warning"
applies = ["weak relationship type", "weak entity type", "line"]
see = ["ER-25", "ER-27", "ER-24"]
+++
**Rule.** In an existence dependency of an entity type on itself, one of the two ends **should** allow at
most one: each chapter is within at most one other chapter.

**What the program does.** A warning, *"each 'Chapter' can only depend on one other 'Chapter', so one of
the two ends has to allow at most one"*, when neither end does.

- "At most one" is `1` or `c` in n-notation, at the end of the one *above* (counted there: how many
  are above each one), and `(1,1)` or `(0,1)` in min-max at the end of the one *below*.
- The end that allows at most one is the owner's end: the program takes the other end as the dependent's.
- A missing label counts as allowing at most one.

**Why.** As every weak entity, a chapter depends on one owner (ER-24): the chapter it is within. If both
ends allowed many, no end would be the owner's.

**Examples.**

```erd violates ER-26
\entity[weak]{chapter}(0,0){Chapter}
\attributes[anchor=west]{chapter}{*Title}
\relationship[dependency=E]{in}(5,0){in}
\link[role=part, double, bend left=30]{chapter}{in}{cn}
\link[role=whole, double, bend right=30]{chapter}{in}{cn}
```

```erd follows
\entity[weak]{chapter}(0,0){Chapter}
\attributes[anchor=west]{chapter}{*Title}
\relationship[dependency=E]{in}(5,0){in}
\link[role=part, double, bend left=30]{chapter}{in}{cn}
\link[role=whole, double, bend right=30]{chapter}{in}{c}
```
