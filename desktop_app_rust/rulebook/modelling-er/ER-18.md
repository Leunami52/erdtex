+++
id = "ER-18"
title = "The roles of one entity type must tell its lines apart"
kind = "warning"
applies = ["relationship type", "PART_OF", "line"]
see = ["ER-17"]
+++
**Rule.** The roles of an entity type that takes part more than once in a relationship type **should**
all be different.

**What the program does.** A warning, *"'X' takes part n times here with the same role; the roles have
to tell the edges apart"*, when every line has a role (otherwise ER-17 applies) but two of them are
equal.

- Roles are compared exactly, after removing spaces at their ends: `Boss` and `boss` are different.

**Why.** Roles exist to tell the lines apart; two equal roles tell nothing.

**Examples.**

```erd violates ER-18
\entity{employee}(0,0){Employee}
\attributes[anchor=west]{employee}{*SSN}
\relationship{supervises}(5,0){supervises}
\link[role=staff, bend left=30]{employee}{supervises}{cn}
\link[role=staff, bend right=30]{employee}{supervises}{c}
```

```erd follows
\entity{employee}(0,0){Employee}
\attributes[anchor=west]{employee}{*SSN}
\relationship{supervises}(5,0){supervises}
\link[role=boss, bend left=30]{employee}{supervises}{cn}
\link[role=staff, bend right=30]{employee}{supervises}{c}
```
