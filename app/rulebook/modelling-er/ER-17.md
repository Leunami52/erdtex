+++
id = "ER-17"
title = "An entity type taking part more than once needs a role on each line"
kind = "warning"
applies = ["relationship type", "PART_OF", "line"]
see = ["ER-18", "ER-8"]
+++
**Rule.** Where an entity type takes part in one relationship type more than once, each of its lines
to it **should** have a role that says which part it plays.

**What the program does.** A warning, *"'X' takes part n times here, so each of these edges needs a
role"*, at the relationship type, when at least one of those lines has no role. A role is set in the
line's *Element* pane (`role=` in the file) and drawn beside the line.

- Only the lines of the entity type that takes part more than once need roles; other lines may have one
  but needn't.
- A role is compared without spaces at its ends; a role of spaces only counts as none.
- Lines through a junction point count as one line to the entity type at its end; the role may be on any
  piece of it.

**Why.** In *Employee supervises Employee*, both lines lead to *Employee*. Without roles there is no
telling which side is the supervisor: the cardinalities, and the relational schema's two foreign keys,
would be ambiguous.

**Examples.**

```erd violates ER-17
\entity{employee}(0,0){Employee}
\attributes[anchor=west]{employee}{*SSN}
\relationship{supervises}(5,0){supervises}
\link[bend left=30]{employee}{supervises}{cn}
\link[bend right=30]{employee}{supervises}{c}
```

```erd follows
\entity{employee}(0,0){Employee}
\attributes[anchor=west]{employee}{*SSN}
\relationship{supervises}(5,0){supervises}
\link[role=boss, bend left=30]{employee}{supervises}{cn}
\link[role=staff, bend right=30]{employee}{supervises}{c}
```
