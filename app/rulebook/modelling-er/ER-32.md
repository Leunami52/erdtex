+++
id = "ER-32"
title = "A generalisation has no attributes"
kind = "warning"
applies = ["IS_A", "CAN_BE", "attribute list"]
see = ["ER-4"]
+++
**Rule.** A generalisation (IS_A or CAN_BE) **should not** have an attribute list.

**What the program does.** A warning, *"a generalisation cannot have attributes"*, at the generalisation,
when an attribute list belongs to it.

- The *Element* pane of a generalisation offers no *Add attribute list*; this happens in a file written
  by hand.

**Why.** A generalisation isn't a set of things of its own: it relates a supertype to its subtypes.
Attributes belong to the supertype (shared by all) or to a subtype (only its entities have them).

**Examples.**

```erd violates ER-32
\entity{person}(0,0){Person}
\attributes{person}{*SSN, Name}
\isa{g}(0,-2){person}{cd}
\attributes{g}{Since}
\entity{employee}(-2,-4){Employee}
\entity{student}(2,-4){Student}
\link{employee}{g}
\link{student}{g}
```

```erd follows
\entity{person}(0,0){Person}
\attributes{person}{*SSN, Name}
\isa{g}(0,-2){person}{cd}
\entity{employee}(-2,-4){Employee}
\entity{student}(2,-4){Student}
\link{employee}{g}
\link{student}{g}
\attributes{student}{Since}
```
