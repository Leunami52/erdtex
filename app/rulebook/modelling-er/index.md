What makes an entity-relationship diagram correct: the rules behind the Problems panel.

In these rules, **must** means the program reports an **error**, **should** a **warning** (or, where the
rule says so, a **hint**: a suggestion, shown only when *Show hints* is on), and **may** that something
is allowed. Each problem in the Problems panel names its rule, and its context menu opens it here.
