How *File → Relational Schema / SQL…* turns an ER diagram into a relational schema: relations, their
columns, primary keys and foreign keys.

In these rules, **becomes** says what an element turns into. Where the mapping could go more than one
way, the rule names the **choices** (the *decisions* in the Relational Schema window) and the one taken
unless you choose another. Each choice is stored on its element, so a diagram keeps it.

In the text form of a schema, `_name_` marks a primary key column, `/name/` a foreign key, and
`_/name/_` a column that is both.
