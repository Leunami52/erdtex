ERDTeX runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you draw, open or save is sent anywhere.

**Stored in this browser only:** your settings (Preferences), and the diagrams open when you last left
the page, so that they come back after a reload. Clearing this site's data in the browser removes them.
No cookies are used, and nothing is stored for any other purpose.

**Nothing else is loaded:** no fonts, scripts or analytics from other sites. After the first visit the
program is kept in the browser, so that later visits load almost nothing and it works offline.

**The host:** the website is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub, like any web host, sees the addresses of
the visitors who load it; see GitHub's privacy statement.
